# project 8 roleplay rulebook

## general rules

### 1  roleplay
all players must remain in character during active roleplay

### 2  random deathmatch
do not kill or attack another player without a valid roleplay reason

### 3  vehicle deathmatch
do not intentionally use a vehicle to hit or kill players without valid roleplay

### 4  fail roleplay
players must act realistically and follow the situation they are involved in

### 5  metagaming
do not use information your character would not realistically know

### 6  powergaming
do not force actions onto another player without giving them a chance to respond

### 7  combat logging
do not leave the server to avoid an active roleplay situation

### 8  exploiting
bugs glitches cheats and exploits are not allowed

## police rules

officers must maintain professional roleplay

officers must follow department procedures and chain of command

police equipment and vehicles should be used appropriately

## civilian rules

civilians must participate in realistic roleplay

do not intentionally interfere with active police or criminal scenes without a roleplay reason

## criminal rules

criminal roleplay must remain realistic

players should not repeatedly start random conflicts without proper roleplay

## punishments

rule violations may result in

warning

kick

temporary ban

permanent ban

the punishment depends on the severity and circumstances of the violation

## final rule

staff may take action against behavior that seriously damages the roleplay experience even if a specific situation is not directly listed above
